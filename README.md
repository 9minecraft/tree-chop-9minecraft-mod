# Tree Chop — Harvest Whole Trees with One Swing

Tree Chop 9minecraft Mod lets you cut down whole trees just by breaking one log. Instead of breaking every block one by one, you hit the trunk with an axe and the rest of the wood falls down. This saves you a lot of time when gathering materials for a big build. You still need a regular axe to do this, so your survival game stays balanced. It works great for normal survival worlds, skyblock islands, and servers with your friends.

You cut down a whole tree by breaking the bottom log with any axe.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **hammertater**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
